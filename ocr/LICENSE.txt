Third-party OCR engine bundled with this site (self-hosted, same origin)

This directory contains an unmodified, self-hosted copy of the browser OCR engine
used by the "材料變自測 / 上傳圖片辨識" feature. All files are served from this
site's own origin; no third-party CDN is contacted at runtime, and images never
leave the user's device for local recognition.
